## 2. The BIOCORE scoring element — everything published

This is the single richest FRC-specific disclosure of the pre-season. Source: [Pre-Orders for 2027 Scoring Elements](https://community.firstinspires.org/2026-pre-orders-for-2027-scoring-elements) (FRC Staff, **June 8, 2026**) and the [AndyMark product page](https://andymark.com/products/biocore-frc-scoring-element-pre-order).

### Directly stated facts `[VERIFIED]`

1. **"BIOCORE has one type of scoring element."** — Single game piece. No multi-piece sorting/matching game at the *object* level.
2. **"Robots will have a holding limit during the match (the holding limit is more than one)."** — A holding limit exists and is ≥ 2.
3. **"All teams will receive a quantity of scoring elements in the Kickoff Kit Season Specific Box greater than the holding limit."**
4. **"No additional scoring elements will be available through FIRST Choice."**
5. **"The scoring element is a custom item manufactured overseas with properties specific to BIOCORE."** Only sources: Kickoff Kit, AndyMark, or AndyMark distributors.
6. Pre-orders opened **June 8, 2026**, run until **Kickoff (Jan 9, 2027)**, ship from **Jan 11, 2027** in order received. Non-returnable, non-cancellable, cannot be combined with other AndyMark orders, payment cannot be changed after receipt.
7. FIRST explicitly compares the sourcing effort to 2026: *"For REBUILT presented by Haas, many hours went into engineering, testing, and sourcing a scoring element that would reliably be processed by the Hub and robots. We have done a similar process for BIOCORE."*

### Part numbers and pricing `[VERIFIED]`

| SKU | Variant label (verbatim from AndyMark) | Price |
|---|---|---|
| `am-5901_kop` | "# included in the Kit of Parts" | **$70.00** |
| `am-5901_partial` | "1/5 of Full Field" | **$169.00** |

Product listed under AndyMark collection **"2027 FIRST® Robotics Competition season BIOCORE™"**; created 2026-06-03, published 2026-06-08. Tags: `preorder`, `Not Returnable`, `New for 2026`.

FIRST states they are **intentionally not disclosing the count** in each quantity. `[VERIFIED]`

### Quantitative inference from pricing `[SPECULATION]`

Assuming price scales linearly with unit count (same per-unit cost and margin across both variants — reasonable for a single injection-molded/custom part sold by the same vendor in the same campaign):

- KoP quantity / (1/5-field quantity) = 70 / 169 = **0.414**
- Full field = 5 × (1/5 field) = 5 / 0.414 ≈ **12.1 × the KoP quantity**
- Full-field cost to a team ≈ 5 × $169 = **$845**

So: **a full field of BIOCORE scoring elements is roughly twelve times what a team gets in its Kickoff Kit.** Combined with fact (3) above (KoP qty > holding limit), this bounds the holding limit: `holding_limit < KoP_qty ≈ full_field / 12`.

If the full field holds ~60 elements, KoP ≈ 5 and holding limit ∈ {2,3,4}. If the full field holds ~100, KoP ≈ 8 and the holding limit could be up to 7. **Practical read: expect a holding limit in the low single digits, most likely 2–5.** Treat this as a planning band, not a fact.

⚠️ **Do NOT infer mass from the AndyMark listing.** Both variants carry an identical Shopify `weight` value of `453139`, despite representing different quantities. Identical weights across differently-sized variants proves the field is a placeholder, not real data. `[VERIFIED]` (observation) / mass remains **unknown**.

## 6. Control system / kit of parts / hardware for 2027 — the biggest real story

**This is the largest actual change facing FRC teams in 2027, and it is unrelated to the game.**

### Systemcore replaces the roboRIO, starting this season

`[VERIFIED]` — [Control System Update – FIRST Tech Challenge Edition](https://community.firstinspires.org/control-system-update-first-tech-challenge-edition): *"the new control system will be available to FIRST Tech Challenge teams to compete with starting in the 2027-28 season (**FIRST Robotics Competition teams will start using the controller in the 2026-27 season**)."*

Systemcore specs `[VERIFIED]` — [SystemCore Alpha Testing – First Wave](https://community.firstinspires.org/systemcore-alpha-testing-first-wave) and [WPILib Systemcore docs](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/latest/docs/software/systemcore-info/systemcore-introduction.html):
- **5.3 in × 2.8 in × 1.1 in** (135.3 mm × 71.5 mm × 28.13 mm), **~0.475 lb (215 g)**
- Onboard **quad-core ARM Cortex-A76**
- Built-in **CAN, I2C, USB, Ethernet**, reconfigurable I/O (PWM, DIO, Analog In)
- **Onboard IMU**, LEDs, and an **integrated diagnostic screen**
- Controller-hosted web programming environments

Rollout status `[VERIFIED]` — [2027 Control System Testing Reminder](https://community.firstinspires.org/2025-control-system-testing-reminder-for-2027) (Oct 7, 2025):
- Alpha wave 1 = **50 FRC teams**, began June 2025; feedback tracked on a public GitHub project
- Ran in **more than 1,000 competition matches** across the 2025 offseason
- FIRST is *"on target to meet our goal of offering Systemcore at a lower price than the roboRIO"* — **exact price still unannounced**
- **Emerson/NI** is working to enable **LabVIEW Community Edition** on Systemcore for both FRC and FTC (cross-platform, simplified licensing)

### New Driver Station application for 2027

`[VERIFIED]` — [The 2027 FIRST Driver Station](https://wpilib.org/blog/the-2027-first-driver-station) (WPILib, **April 24, 2026**):
- Ground-up rewrite, shared across FRC and FTC, integrates WPILib's new **Op Modes**
- Runs on **Windows / macOS / Linux**, each on **x64 and arm64**
- **FMS support is Windows-only** (plus a future hardware appliance). macOS and Linux **will not** get FMS support — meaning **you cannot use a Mac or Linux laptop as a competition driver station**
- Minimum display: **1280 px effective width after scaling**; baseline hardware is the existing KOP laptop (**HP 14-dq6011dx**)
- **Incompatible with roboRIO** and current FTC SDK devices — Systemcore only
- Currently **closed source**; repo at [wpilibsuite/FirstDriverStation-Public](https://github.com/wpilibsuite/FirstDriverStation-Public)

> **Action item, pre-kickoff:** confirm the team's driver-station laptop is Windows and meets the 1280 px width requirement. A Mac-based drive team is a hard blocker in 2027.

### FIRST A301 smart actuator — legal in FRC starting 2027

`[VERIFIED]` — [Introducing the FIRST A301](https://community.firstinspires.org/introducing-the-first-a301) (Dec 9, 2025): *"We plan that the FIRST A301 will also be available and legal for use in FIRST Robotics Competition starting in the 2027 season."*

- Brushless, does **both** open-loop motor and closed-loop servo emulation
- **CAN** comms + power over a single **4-pin Molex Micro-Fit+**
- **15T spline output, 8 mm round profile** (explicitly called "an established standard in the FIRST Robotics Competition ecosystem")
- **Modular swappable gearboxes**, ratios spanning **100–6,000 RPM**, sold separately
- **Integrated absolute encoder on the output**
- **30 W peak**, 24 V–12 V voltage compensation
- **38 mm × 28 mm × 81 mm**
- Manufactured exclusively by **REV Robotics** for FIRST; sold through the FIRST Storefront and approved resellers
- Pricing/availability: *"available during the 2026 offseason"* — **not yet published**
- Preliminary CAD published on Onshape (STEP downloadable)

**Important scoping note:** the A301's *exclusivity* mandate ("the only legal actuator") applies to **FTC** with the new control system in 2027-28. For FRC it is described as **"available and legal"** — additive, not exclusive. Nothing published suggests FRC's existing motor ecosystem is being restricted in 2027. `[VERIFIED]` on the wording / `[SPECULATION]` that FRC motor rules are otherwise unchanged.

## Open questions the manual will answer

Concrete unknowns, ordered by how much they change design and strategy decisions. Use this as the diff checklist on 2027-01-09.

### Scoring element (highest leverage — everything downstream depends on it)
1. **Geometry:** sphere, ellipsoid, torus, disc, cube, or irregular? The "core/nucleus" logo motif and Chief Delphi's football/kernel reading are the only (weak) signals.
2. **Major dimension and mass.** Determines intake width, hopper volume, shooter/roller sizing, and whether COG shifts matter.
3. **Material, surface, compliance, coefficient of restitution.** Is it compressible? Does it bounce? Grippy or slick?
4. **Color(s)** — one color or alliance-colored? Palette suggests yellow `#F7E326`.
5. **Exact count in the KoP Season Specific Box**, and the **exact holding limit** (published only as ">1"). My band: 2–5. Confirm immediately — it caps every hopper design.
6. **Exact full-field count.** My linear-pricing inference says ≈12× the KoP quantity; verify.
7. Is it **oriented** (does pose matter for scoring), or orientation-agnostic?

### Game structure
8. What does "**delve into the heart of what sustains life on Earth**" map to mechanically? "Core" implies a **central field structure** — is there a shared/contested center element?
9. **Is the holding limit enforced by rule, by physical goal design, or both?** A rule-enforced limit >1 usually implies a fast cycle game, not a hoarding game.
10. **Autonomous** structure: duration, points, and whether there is a 2026-style leave/auto-scoring split.
11. **Endgame**: climb vs. platform vs. neither. Chief Delphi notes the last three seasons were climbs and the last platform games were 2019/2023 — a platform return is overdue. `[SPECULATION]`
12. **Ranking-point structure** and coopertition mechanics.
13. **Human player** involvement — loading stations, scoring from the field border, and whether human players can score directly.
14. Is there a **defense-limiting** zone (protected zone) mechanic?

### Field
15. Field layout within the confirmed **30 ft × 74 ft** carpeted footprint. What is at center, and what is in the alliance zones?
16. Are there **non-flat elements** (ramps, terrain, barriers)? Chief Delphi speculation about elevation from the CANOPY teaser is unverified.
17. Are field elements **actuated/dynamic** (like 2026's Hub, which "processed" the scoring element) — the REBUILT comparison in the scoring-element blog is a hint that BIOCORE may again have a mechanism that must reliably process the game piece. `[SPECULATION]`
18. **Team-buildable field element** availability, cost, and drawings.

### Rules and manual philosophy
19. **Does the FRC manual adopt the FTC rewrite philosophy** — intent-based rules, referee discretion, escalation path, and a **Competition Integrity Contract**? If yes, the loophole surface shifts from text gaps to discretion ambiguity.
20. Does FRC adopt the **evergreen (green/asterisk) vs. season-specific (orange)** rule coloring? That would make year-over-year diffing dramatically easier.
21. **Robot size/weight envelope** and expansion limits — FTC explicitly re-introduced expansion limits for BIOBUZZ. Does FRC follow?
22. **Motor rules**: is the A301 additive only, or does FRC begin restricting the motor list?
23. **Bumper rules** and any 2027-specific changes.

### Control system (resolve these before kickoff, not after)
24. **Is Systemcore mandatory or optional for FRC 2027?** Published wording is *"teams will start using the controller in the 2026-27 season"* — that reads mandatory, but FTC explicitly gets a legacy/hybrid transition and no equivalent FRC transition statement has been published. **This is the single most important unresolved logistical question.**
25. **Systemcore price and order lead time.**
26. Wiring/electrical rules under Systemcore: is the roboRIO legal as a fallback in 2027?
27. **Driver-station laptop:** confirm Windows + ≥1280 px effective width. Mac/Linux cannot run FMS.
28. A301 pricing, available gearbox ratios at launch, and FRC legality wording.

### Logistics
29. Full **2027 event list** — which Regionals return, the new Netherlands Regional, and District schedules (publishing ~September 2026).
30. 2027 **MySite shipping costs** (2026 figures are placeholders).
31. 2027 **Black Tote / Drive Base opt-out voucher values** (2026: $125 + $125).
32. Whether **Week 7** changes advancement math, Championship allocation, or district points.
